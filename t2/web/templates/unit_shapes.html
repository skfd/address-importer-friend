These addr:flats values are over OSM's 255-character tag limit, and a truncated listing would assert the building stops at whatever unit the cut landed on. Nothing compresses a suffixed or stepped sequence, so the listing is dropped and the building is uploaded without it. A collapse verdict cannot make it fit; picking civic-only records that you agree.
